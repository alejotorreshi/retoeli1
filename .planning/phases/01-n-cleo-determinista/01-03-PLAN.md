<objective>
Produce the formulario in the client's format: xlsx at the exact sheet and cell with visible marks, a PDF in template order with marks and notes, and, for portal cases, `valores-portal.md` with ok:true and the "formato no soportado" notice. Every value is re-read from the maestro after the received rutas-only mapping is checked against the canonical recompute (D-11).

Purpose: this is HU-3 and the design-level proof of CA2. The caller can only pass rutas and states, so a model cannot get an invented or tampered value into a signed form.
Output: `proveedor_generar_formulario` for all three formats, with golden readbacks (exceljs for xlsx, the content-stream extractor for pdf, file text for portal) and contract tests for tampering and reprocessing.
</objective>
