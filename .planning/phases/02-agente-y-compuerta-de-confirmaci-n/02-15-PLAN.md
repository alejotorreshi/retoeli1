<objective>
Close UAT gap G-02-3 (major; the same defect as IN-06 in 02-REVIEW-DISPOSITION.md). After a successful send, a blocked simular_envio retry by the model re-opens the already-sent case as pending, and the next "envía" writes ENVIO-SIMULADO.md a second time. The cause, per .planning/debug/reenvio-caso-ya-enviado.md: the gate has no "sent" state. aplicarEvento in compuerta.policy.ts turns every BLOQUEADO event of a case listed in `conocidos` back into a pending case, and sesion.paquetesConocidos keeps the case after ENVIADO.

User decision (locked; recorded here as reading D-63.4 of 02-CONTEXT.md): a blocked retry NEVER re-opens a case already sent in this session. A NEW successful armar_paquete (ARMADO) for that case DOES open a new cycle: it clears the sent mark and asks for confirmation again, consistent with ENV-02, D-37, D-42 and phase-1 D-12. The case stays in `conocidos`, because per-case log routing (casosDeBitacora, CA4) and the gate_* lines depend on it. CONFIRMACION_REQUERIDA and its CA4 log line stay unchanged.

Purpose: re-close the "una sola vez y solo para ese caso" half of ENV-02 and ROADMAP Phase 2 criterion 2. It also makes ENV-03's needsConfirmation honest after a send, and gives DEMO-03 tests that would have caught the defect.
Output: CasosEnviados, enviadosTrasEventos, the sent-aware cerrarTurno, Sesion.casosEnviados, unit rows, the rewritten AGT-04 loop test, the same-turn variant, the re-arm control, and the D-63.4 and STATE.md decision records.
</objective>
