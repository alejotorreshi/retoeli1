<objective>
Make every turn bounded, recoverable and observable. Caps on iterations, session tokens and turn time always end with a clear answer. Provider failures are retried, can switch to the fallback model, and otherwise show a Spanish message while the session stays alive. Every step leaves a correlated JSON log line with no key and no PII.

Purpose: AGT-01 and CA1 (cap with what was done and what is missing), LLM-05 (configurable caps with clear messages), LLM-03 and LLM-04 (decorators composed in the real agent), AGT-04 and AGT-05 (CA5, sessions surviving errors and aborts) and SEG-03 (correlated logs). This closes ROADMAP criteria 3, 4 (caps) and 5 (logs, fake-LLM coverage).
Output: budget and cap handling, the deadline and abort handling, the resilience and logger composition in crearAgente, and five loop test suites.
</objective>
