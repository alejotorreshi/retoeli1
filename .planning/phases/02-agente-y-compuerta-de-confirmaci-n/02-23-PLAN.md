<objective>
Close the remaining class of gap G-02-2 (02-UAT.md) that plan 02-22 closed only for its quoted phrases. Findings addressed: CR-04 (blocker), WR-19, WR-20, WR-21 and WR-22 of 02-REVIEW.md; IN-28 (with threat T-02-71), IN-29 and IN-30(a); truths 31, 38 and 41 and the three gaps of 02-VERIFICATION.md (status gaps_found 39/42).

Root cause (binding, from 02-REVIEW.md CR-04 and 02-VERIFICATION.md gap 1): rule (b) of D-63.8 accepts any non-question clause made only of NEGACIONES and RELLENO words. Four things combine: PATRON_CLAUSULA cuts the head of a question into its own clause ("Luego, ¿qué sigue?" and "Luego ¿qué sigue?" give the clause "luego"); separar removes the case slug before the clause is evaluated ("para pe-andes" becomes "para"); RELLENO admits articles and nouns ("el", "caso", "paquete"); and connectors ("para", "luego", "después", "mejor", "momento", "nada") count as a rejection core on their own. Form (a) also reads accent-stripped words, so the preterite "envié" equals the subjunctive "envie" (WR-20), and it accepts "hagas", "confirmo" and "autorizo" with any object (WR-21). The result: common questions and requests cancel every pending case, the model says the case is still pending, and the next "sí" is blocked with CONFIRMACION_REQUERIDA — the double-confirmation symptom the user treated as blocking (HV2). Ten questions regressed against the 02-22 base (10a43ad). In the other direction, explicit rejections outside the twelve exact forms ("no quiero enviarlo todavía", "espera un momento", "no lo confirmes", "todavía no qué falta?") give otro and the server asks to confirm right after the analyst said not to send (WR-19). The contract tests pinned only quoted examples and short fragments, never the prose rule, so all of this passed green (IN-29).

The refined rule, recorded as D-63.9 (narrows and completes D-63.8). The planner verified it on a scratch copy of the repo: the generated corpus gives 0 violations on the new rule against 1472 false cancels, 221 missed rejections and 1009 question regressions on the current code; the differential is clean; 600 KB adversarial input runs in at most 40 ms against 6.5 to 10 s today; and the full suite passes apart from the single structural assertion this plan retargets. A rejection is looked for only in what the analyst affirms, never in what she asks:
- Segmentation: clauses are cut at ".", ",", ";", "!", "?", "…" and before "¿". ":" no longer cuts, so words quoted after ":" are not the analyst's answer (IN-30(a)). A clause with "?" or "¿" is a question. If it does not open with "¿", the words before its first interrogative ("qué", "cuál", "cómo", "cuándo", "dónde", "quién", "cuánto"…) form a separate affirmed clause, so "todavía no qué falta?" rejects and "luego qué sigue?" does not.
- (a) Send negation in an affirmed clause: a negator, then optional unstressed pronouns, then:
  - a send subjunctive ("envíes", "envíe", "mandes"…), with any complement; or
  - a generic verb ("hagas", "confirmo", "confirmes", "autorizo", "autorices"…); or
  - a modal ("quiero", "puedo", "vayas", "ocurra"…), an optional "a" or "que", optional pronouns, and a send, confirm or authorize form.
  The generic and modal paths count only when the rest of the clause is cases, NEGACIONES or RELLENO words. A verb written with a final "é" is a preterite and never counts.
- (b) Cancellation: an affirmed clause with "cancela", "cancelar", "cancelo" or "cancelado" whose other words are cases, NEGACIONES or RELLENO.
- (c) Wait clause: an affirmed clause with no case, made only of NEGACIONES and the wait fillers ("por", "favor", "porfa", "ya", "ahora", "gracias", "entonces", "un"), with at least one rejection core word ("no", "nunca", "jamás", "tampoco", "espera"…, "cancela"…, "detente", "detén", "alto", "aguarda"). It does not count when another affirmed clause of the message names a case, because the text rejection cancels every pending case.
- Whole-message wait: a message with no question and no case, made only of NEGACIONES and the wait fillers, with at least one NEGACIONES word ("Para.", "mejor después", "un momento", "luego").
- Everything else is otro: the case stays pending and the server asks again (D-63.6). The confirmation grammar, RELLENO and NEGACIONES do not change, so the set of confirming phrases is unchanged.

Purpose: make the D-63.6 promise hold for the whole class, not for sample phrases, and keep the model's instructions identical to what the server enforces (ENV-02, ENV-03, AGT-02, DEMO-03).
