<objective>
Make the provider adapter resilient and honest: every failure becomes a typed, key-free ErrorLlm, and three small decorators add the per-call timeout, retry with backoff and jitter, and the primary-to-fallback switch. All of it is proven with injected fetch, sleep and randomness, so no test waits or spends tokens.

Purpose: LLM-03 (timeout, retry only on transient errors, 402 as "saldo agotado"), LLM-04 (fallback decorator), LLM-02 (capability flags verified at the body level) and AGT-05 (every kind maps to the Spanish catalog). The composition inside crearAgente happens in plan 02-07.
Output: the complete error mapper, the hardened adapter, three decorators and their unit tests, plus the spike fixtures.
</objective>
