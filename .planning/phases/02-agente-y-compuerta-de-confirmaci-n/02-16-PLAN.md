1. Messages made only of acknowledgements ("ok", "listo", "vale", "claro", "correcto", "ok ok") confirm through an acknowledgement-only branch that plan 02-14 added to esConfirmacionClara.
2. "si" without an accent plus filler ("si todo bien") confirms, because sinDiacriticos removes the accent before AFIRMACIONES is consulted, so the conditional "si" and the affirmation "sí" become the same word.

Diagnosis: .planning/debug/acuse-suelto-confirma-envio.md.

User decisions (locked, UAT H2 "Endurecer", recorded here as reading D-63.5 of 02-CONTEXT.md):
- Remove the acknowledgement-only branch. Confirming requires at least one strong affirmation or send verb, and acknowledgements stay allowed words that never suffice alone.
- "sí" WITH an accent is a strong affirmation anywhere. It is detected before sinDiacriticos, on NFC and lowercase text, so "SÍ" and the NFD form work.
- "si" WITHOUT an accent confirms ONLY when it is the entire message, punctuation ignored. In any other phrase it never suffices: "si todo bien", "si gracias" and "si por favor" give otro, and "si, envía" confirms because of the verb.

Purpose: re-close the classifier half of ENV-02 (ROADMAP Phase 2 criterion 2) with DEMO-03 rows and a loop test. ENV-03's question text does not change: EJEMPLOS_CONFIRMACION ("envía", "sí") still confirms.
