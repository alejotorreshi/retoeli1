## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-02-26 | Denial of service | session corruption on failure or abort | medium | mitigate | Steps are committed atomically, a finally-commit runs on abandonment, and incomplete steps are discarded. historialIntegro runs on every request in the resiliencia tests. | T-02-SC | Tampering | npm installs | high | accept | No package installed in this plan. |
