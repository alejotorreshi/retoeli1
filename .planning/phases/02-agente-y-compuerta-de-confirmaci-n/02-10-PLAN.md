<objective>
Close verification gaps 1 and 2 (code review CR-01 and CR-02) together, because they are the same failure: the gate decided who may send, but not what the analyst confirmed.

Per D-44 the safety net belongs to the server. When a case is pending, the turn now always ends with the server's deterministic question, which names the case and lists the readable bloqueos. Any trailing question the model wrote is removed first, so the next "sí" or "envía" can only answer server-written text. Per D-39 ("ante la duda, otro"), the classifier stops treating the noun "envío", other non-imperative forms of the verb, and weak acknowledgements followed by filler as an explicit confirmation. The question text now promises exactly what the classifier does.

Purpose: re-close ENV-02 and ENV-03 (ROADMAP Phase 2 criterion 2) and make the DEMO-03 gate tests catch these defects. The live test gets the stricter assertion here and runs once in plan 02-13, after every gap is closed.
Output: cierreConPregunta, the retired model-trusting predicate, the stricter classifier, the aligned question texts, and loop, unit and live tests.
</objective>
