<objective>
Close UAT gap 1 (major): the empty state with the case cards opens scrolled to the bottom (scrollTop 183/183 at 1280x720 and 459 at 375x667), so the heading and, at 375, the CO and EC cards are out of view. Per the diagnosis in .planning/debug/estado-vacio-abre-al-fondo.md, the follow-to-end of the timeline starts stuck (seguimientoInicial.pegado true) and the ResizeObserver of the content calls seguirAlFinal on its first notification even when the content is EstadoVacio. Fix direction (from the UAT, kept exactly): follow-to-end does not act while there are no turns, and the follow state resets when the timeline returns to empty. The rule goes into the pure reducer module (FE-02) and the hook only wires it. This keeps D-111 (the empty state shows the cards above the chips, visible from the top) and FE-06 (behavior as before the refactor).

Purpose: FE-06 says the refactor changes no behavior; before 05-09 the empty state was seen from the top.
Output: the turn-aware follow rule with pure tests, the hook wiring, and a DOM regression with simulated heights.
</objective>
