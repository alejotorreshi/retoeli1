<objective>
Migrate the containers of the conversation: the composer, the quick-action chips, the timeline and the empty state it contains (D-100, D-119). Their hooks read their own slice from the store with named selectors and get their callbacks from the actions (D-101), so the screen in `app.tsx` stops threading props through them. The empty state becomes its own component, unchanged in look and copy for now; plan 05-09 adds the case cards to it.

Purpose: these are the components with the most logic in the front (keyboard handling, auto-scroll, per-turn derivations). Moving them with the characterization suite green is the core evidence for FE-06.
Output: four component folders, the legacy files deleted, app.tsx simplified, and the chip assertions of tests/web/acciones-rapidas.test.ts turned into DOM behavior.
</objective>
